# Cornering Aelfa

## Setting

While part of the party is drawn away from the manor yard, two rough-looking men corner the scullery girl Aelfa against the kitchen wall, out of easy sight of the main yard. They're not local - travel-worn, armed, and clearly here for her specifically, not looting. If the party doesn't intervene within a few rounds, they'll frighten her into silence (or worse) and vanish the way they came.

## Monsters

- **1x Thug (CR 1/2, 100 XP)** - the senior of the two, doing the talking and the threatening. Multiattack with a mace; Pack Tactics when his partner is nearby.
- **1x Bandit (CR 1/8, 25 XP)** - hangs back near the alley mouth watching for anyone approaching, ready to cut off Aelfa's retreat or the party's approach with a scimitar or light crossbow.

Total raw XP 125, comfortably inside the level 1 / party-of-4 medium budget of 200 XP once the two-monster multiplier is applied.

## Tactics

The Bandit tries to block the party's approach or line of sight for a round or two while the Thug pressures Aelfa verbally first ("you didn't see anything, understand?") before resorting to force - giving the party a narrow window to intervene before it turns violent. If the fight turns clearly against them, both will disengage and flee toward the treeline rather than fight to the death; neither is loyal enough to the job to die for it.

## Story hooks

Neither man is a noble or a claimant themselves - they're hired muscle, consistent with the fire itself: hired men, not personal enemies. Captured or intimidated into talking, either can confirm someone paid them to make sure Aelfa didn't describe what she saw, without naming who - a lead, not an answer.

## Treasure

A few silver on each body/prisoner and a scrap of oiled cloth that smells faintly of the same accelerant used on the tithe barn - physical corroboration, if the party thinks to compare it, that these two are tied to the fire itself.
