name: Hollowmere Manor & Tithe Barn
type: Manor Yard & Burned Barn
description: Smoke still curls from the blackened ribs of the tithe barn, its roof collapsed inward in a heap of char and ash. The manor house beside it wears a scorch scar up one whole wall, shutters warped from heat, and a cracked windowpane weeping soot-streaked rain. Villagers move through the wet ash in twos and threes, salvaging what they can - a dented cauldron, a half-burned ledger, a child's shoe - while a knot of them argues in low, frightened voices near the well. The air smells of wet char and tallow smoke, and every so often a timber settles somewhere in the wreckage with a groan that makes people flinch.
challenges:
  hazards: |
    - Unstable Roof Beam: a charred rafter still bridges part of the barn's collapsed roof; disturbing the wreckage or a loud noise nearby can bring it down (DC 12 Dexterity save or 1d6 bludgeoning damage and pinned until freed)
    - Smoldering Hot Spots: patches of ash still glow underneath; searching by hand risks a DC 10 Dexterity save or 1d4 fire damage
  puzzles: "- Reading the Wreckage: a DC 12 Investigation check among the ash and timber finds oil residue near the barn's side door and a forced lock - evidence the fire was set, not accidental"
secrets:
  - The forced lock and oil residue point to deliberate arson, not misfortune - whoever set it wanted the tithe rolls destroyed, not just the barn.
  - The manor's family chapel, untouched by the fire, holds an old parish record naming the last uncontested heir before the succession dispute began - if anyone thinks to look there.
plot_hooks:
  - The destroyed tithe rolls are the exact records both rival claimants to Hollowmere would need to press or refute their claim - convenient timing the reeve can't stop worrying at.
  - A half-burned page salvaged from the barn bears a fragment of old script that doesn't match any local hand - a first, faint thread toward the forgotten Roman-era site and its hearth-stone relic, if the party thinks to ask around about it later.
npcs:
  - name: Reeve Ordric
    description: The village reeve - a stooped, soot-streaked man in his fifties, hoarse from shouting orders through the night. Frightened less of the fire itself than of the baron's steward arriving before he has answers, he's the party's first and most desperate source of help.
environmental_features:
  - The half-collapsed barn frame can be climbed or braced for a better vantage over the yard, at the risk of the unstable roof beam above.
  - The manor well is still working and could be used to douse remaining hot spots or draw water for the villagers, buying goodwill.
additional_notes: This is the party's very first scene - let Reeve Ordric's fear and the villagers' quiet grief set the tone before anything mechanical happens. If the party doesn't think to investigate the wreckage itself, Ordric can nervously mention the oil smell and the forced lock himself rather than the scene stalling. Keep the succession dispute and the relic thread as background texture here, not exposition dumps - they surface more directly in later scenes.
