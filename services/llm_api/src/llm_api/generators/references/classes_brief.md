# SRD Classes — Brief Reference

| Class | Hit Die | Primary Ability | Saves | Armor | Spellcasting |
|-------|---------|----------------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Barbarian | d12 | Strength | Str, Con | Light, Medium, Shields | — |
| Bard | d8 | Charisma | Dex, Cha | Light | Cha, known list |
| Cleric | d8 | Wisdom | Wis, Cha | Light, Medium, Shields | Wis, prepared |
| Druid | d8 | Wisdom | Int, Wis | Light, Medium (nonmetal) | Wis, prepared |
| Fighter | d10 | Str or Dex | Str, Con | All | — |
| Monk | d8 | Dex & Wis | Str, Dex | None | — |
| Paladin | d10 | Str & Cha | Wis, Cha | All | Cha, prepared (half level) |
| Ranger | d10 | Dex & Wis | Str, Dex | Light, Medium | Wis, known list |
| Rogue | d8 | Dexterity | Dex, Int | Light | — |
| Sorcerer | d6 | Charisma | Con, Cha | None | Cha, known list |
| Warlock | d8 | Charisma | Wis, Cha | Light | Cha, Pact Magic (short rest) |
| Wizard | d6 | Intelligence | Int, Wis | None | Int, prepared from spellbook |

## Proficiency Bonus by Level
1–4: +2 | 5–8: +3 | 9–12: +4 | 13–16: +5 | 17–20: +6

## HP Calculation
Level 1: max hit die + Con mod. Level 2+: average (d6=4, d8=5, d10=6, d12=7) + Con mod per level.

## Key Tier Features
- **Tier 1 (1–4):** Class feature, subclass at 3 (most classes), ASI at 4
- **Tier 2 (5–10):** Extra Attack (martials), 3rd-level spells (full casters)
- **Tier 3 (11–16):** 6th-level spells, third Extra Attack (Fighter 11)
- **Tier 4 (17–20):** 9th-level spells, capstone features at 20

## ASI Levels
All classes: 4, 8, 12, 16, 19. Fighter extra: 6, 14. Rogue extra: 10.
