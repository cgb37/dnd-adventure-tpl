## HP Calculation

- **Level 1**: Hit die maximum + Constitution modifier
- **Level 2+**: Roll hit die (or take average) + Constitution modifier per level
- **Average per die**: d6=4, d8=5, d10=6, d12=7

## Key Class Features by Tier

### Tier 1 (Levels 1-4)
- **Barbarian**: Rage, Reckless Attack, Primal Path (3)
- **Bard**: Spellcasting, Bardic Inspiration, Bard College (3), Expertise
- **Cleric**: Spellcasting, Divine Domain (1), Channel Divinity (2)
- **Druid**: Spellcasting, Wild Shape (2), Druid Circle (2)
- **Fighter**: Fighting Style, Second Wind, Action Surge (2), Martial Archetype (3)
- **Monk**: Martial Arts, Ki, Monastic Tradition (3), Deflect Missiles
- **Paladin**: Divine Sense, Lay on Hands, Fighting Style (2), Spellcasting (2), Sacred Oath (3)
- **Ranger**: Favored Enemy, Natural Explorer, Fighting Style (2), Spellcasting (2), Ranger Archetype (3)
- **Rogue**: Sneak Attack, Expertise, Cunning Action (2), Roguish Archetype (3)
- **Sorcerer**: Spellcasting, Sorcerous Origin (1), Font of Magic (2), Metamagic (3)
- **Warlock**: Pact Magic, Otherworldly Patron (1), Eldritch Invocations (2), Pact Boon (3)
- **Wizard**: Spellcasting, Arcane Recovery, Arcane Tradition (2)

### Tier 2 (Levels 5-10)
Key milestones: Extra Attack (5 for martials), 3rd-level spells (5 for full casters), Uncanny Dodge (Rogue 5)

### Tier 3 (Levels 11-16)
Key milestones: 6th-level spells (11 for full casters), third Extra Attack (Fighter 11), Reliable Talent (Rogue 11)

### Tier 4 (Levels 17-20)
Key milestones: 9th-level spells (17 for full casters), capstone features (20)
